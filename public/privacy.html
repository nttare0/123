Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Who to contact

DropTV is available at https://droptv.vercel.app/. For privacy questions or requests, contact nttare0@gmail.com. This policy describes the current application; it does not promise that infrastructure providers collect no data.

Country suggestions without GPS

When automatic country selection is enabled, your browser requests /api/country from this website. Vercel estimates your country from the IP address used to connect and supplies a country code to the app. We do not request GPS, browser location permission or your precise address, and do not send a separate lookup to an external geolocation API. A country code is held in memory for the current page load. VPNs, proxies and mobile networks can make the estimate wrong. Choose a country in Settings to override automatic detection; when a manual country is set, the app does not initiate a new lookup. If the estimate fails, worldwide channels are shown.

On-device preferences

Favourites, recent channel IDs and viewing timestamps, playback settings, selected country and theme are stored in browser local storage. These records are not uploaded by the app or attached to an account. Clear them through your browser site-data controls. Anyone using the same browser profile may be able to see them.

Hosting and third parties

Requests to our host, Vercel, necessarily include network information such as an IP address and browser headers; Vercel may retain operational or security logs according to its policies. When you play a stream or load a channel logo, your browser connects directly to its provider. That provider receives network information and may use its own cookies or tracking technologies. Third-party links and media are governed by their providers’ policies; we do not control their retention or use.

Analytics, retention and your choices

The current app does not include advertising trackers, Google Analytics or an account system. Google Search Console is a webmaster tool, not an analytics tracker installed in your browser. Local preferences remain until cleared or replaced. We do not maintain a separate server database of viewing history. Email correspondence and provider-managed operational logs may have different retention periods. You may contact us about access, correction or deletion where applicable; we cannot remove data held independently by broadcasters or your browser without your action.

Updates

We will update this notice if our data practices change. Depending on where you live, additional privacy rights may apply, including contacting your local data-protection authority. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information in support emails.
