Cookies and Local Storage

Last updated: 6 November 2026

What Zepvi stores

Cookies and local storage are different browser technologies. The app uses local storage to remember useful preferences; it does not install advertising or analytics cookies. This does not mean the host or third-party media providers can never use cookies.

Local storage inventory

live-tv-hub:settings stores country preference, autoplay, failover, header filtering, volume and mute settings. live-tv-hub:library stores favourites and recent channel IDs with timestamps. live-tv-hub:theme stores the colour theme. These keys retain the legacy app name for compatibility. Values persist on this browser until cleared or overwritten.

Sidebar and network lookup

The sidebar component may set a sidebar_state preference cookie when the sidebar is toggled, with a lifetime of up to seven days. Automatic country lookup uses the current connection’s IP-country estimate through Vercel, not a location cookie. The app keeps that result in memory rather than persistent storage.

Third-party media

Playing a stream or loading its artwork connects directly to another provider. Providers may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies and your browser settings. Zepvi does not control those technologies. Avoid playback or restrict third-party cookies through your browser if you do not want those connections.

Your controls

You can clear Zepvi cookies and site data in your browser, block cookies or local storage, change country in Settings, and clear favourites or recent history in the app. Blocking storage can prevent preferences from being saved. The app includes Vercel Web Analytics, which is designed to work without analytics cookies. Page-view URLs have query strings and fragments removed before sending. We have not added advertising trackers or an “Accept all” advertising banner. Consent and disclosure requirements vary by jurisdiction; the operator must review the analytics configuration and add consent controls where required. Questions: nttare0@gmail.com.

Spotlight discovery

Recent movie metadata is fetched directly by your browser from Cinemeta without an API key. Cinemeta receives your IP address and browser request headers. Requests omit credentials. Results are cached in memory for up to 30 minutes, not stored in cookies or local storage. Poster images also load directly from provider image servers, which receive your network information. Clicking a title opens IMDb under its own privacy policy. Spotlight does not add analytics or tracking cookies.

Installed app and offline screen

A service worker caches only a small offline screen, its icon and the favicon using browser Cache Storage. Live streams, API responses and analytics are not cached by this worker. Cached assets remain until replaced or you clear site data. Installing Zepvi uses your browser’s own installation controls; no download button is added to the site.
